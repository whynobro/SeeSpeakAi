# Business model

Decided Sat Nov 14 (Michael). One model only. Sources for prices are in `demo-script.md` and `../research.md`.

## Who pays, who uses it

| Who                                 | Pays               | Gets                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Family or learner                   | $0                 | Practice, levels and tiers, progress, the bird and its outfits                         |
| Speech therapist or school district | $120 a seat a year | Sends practice by link, gets summaries back, tracks many students, adult override code |

- **The payer:** the school district's special education office, one seat per speech therapist or teacher of the
  deaf.
- **Why $120:** Little Bee Speech charges therapists $119.99 a year. SLP Toolkit charges districts $235 a seat.
- **Built today:** the therapist practice link, the learner assign page and the summary back (#98). The adult
  override code is built (the code stays on the device; overrides never change the measured numbers).

## Why free for families is good business

- Families never hit a paywall, so more students practice between sessions.
- More practice means more summaries reaching therapists. That is what a district pays for.
- Free families are how it spreads. Therapists and districts are who pays.
- The free app costs almost nothing to run: all the sound measuring happens on the phone, and no audio is
  uploaded or stored.

## Judge questions

- **"What if no therapist pays?"** "The family app costs almost nothing to run, because the sound work happens on
  the phone. The paid part is the therapist side: assigning practice, seeing summaries, many students at once.
  That's what saves a therapist with 50 students time."
- **"Why would they switch?"** "Today there is no feedback between sessions. With SeeSpeak the therapist sends
  practice by link and sees how it went."
- **"Where do the first users come from?"** Only groups we have really spoken to. See below.
