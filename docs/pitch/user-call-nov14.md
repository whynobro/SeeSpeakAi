# User call, Sat Nov 14 (AI Warriors Zoom)

Holly, Michael and Mason showed the app on a video call to Yuka, a former member of the Cal Poly ASL Club. She
learned ASL in high school and has deaf friends. **She gave us permission to record the call and use it** for the
project (pitch and demo video), per Michael, Nov 14.

- Recording (42 min video, audio) and Zoom's summary: GitHub release
  [user-call-nov14](https://github.com/whynobro/SeeSpeakAi/releases/tag/user-call-nov14). Kept out of git
  history because of size. Also in the team OneDrive.
- The summary is AI-generated by Zoom, so names and details may be slightly off, and it paraphrases. Pull exact
  quotes from the recording.

**How to pitch her:** a likely user from the Deaf and hard-of-hearing community's circle who gave us feedback. Not
"a deaf user", and not the club president. Her feedback shaped the app (see below), which is our proof that we
talked to likely users.

## What she told us

- The app may suit **children** (about kindergarten to 3rd grade) more than deaf adults, with a **parent and child
  doing it together**.
- **Partner with speech therapists:** let them assign homework and lessons to their clients.
- Visual learning matters most: **mouth pictures** and video, because deaf users rely on visual and touch cues
  (vibration), not sound.
- It may be popular with **speech impediments in general**, not only deaf and hard-of-hearing learners.
- Add **"r" and "th"** sounds.
- Train on **many voices and accents**, including the Deaf accent.
- **Gamify it:** rewards such as confetti when a sound is right.
- Resources she named: ADWAS and Lifeprint.
- She offered to connect us with people in the Deaf and hard-of-hearing community and the medical field for
  testing and feedback.

## What we built because of it (this weekend)

| Her feedback                     | What we built                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Partner with speech therapists   | Therapist practice link, assign page and summary back (#98)     |
| Gamify with rewards and confetti | Levels and tiers (#90, #103), ding and confetti (#96), the bird |
| Mouth pictures and video         | Mouth pictures and the how-to video on practice                 |
| "r" and "th"                     | Not scored yet: shown as "show how, no score" only              |
| Many voices, Deaf accent         | Scoring measures the hiss, not words; tuned on real voices      |
