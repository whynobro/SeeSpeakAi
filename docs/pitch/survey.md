# Feedback survey and post text

Owner: Holly. Status: DRAFT. Live form: https://forms.gle/ZRWoTHGfT6QrjE3w7 (Holly's Google account, published Sat Nov 14). The questions below are what the form contains. About 3 minutes to answer. No names, no email, nobody under 18.

Form settings: do not collect email addresses, do not require sign-in, no required questions except the first two.

## Form title and opening

**SeeSpeak: 3 minutes of your opinion on a speech practice idea**

We are three Cal Poly students at a weekend hackathon. We are building a free practice tool for hard-of-hearing people who _choose_ to work on spoken English. We would like your honest opinion, including "this is a bad idea".

The idea: you say a word to your phone, and the screen shows you whether a high sound like "s" or "sh" was there. A meter moves while you speak, and the result is shown with a color, a word and a vibration, never by sound alone. The words to practice are picked from your own hearing test. Your voice stays on your phone. It is not therapy, and its quick hearing check is not a medical hearing test. Sign language is welcome, and nobody should feel they have to practice speech.

This form is anonymous. We do not ask for your name or email. Please answer only if you are 18 or older. We will share totals and short anonymous comments in our pitch on Sunday, Nov 15.

## Questions

1. **I am 18 or older and I agree to share my answers anonymously.** (Yes. Required. If not, the form ends.)
2. **Which of these describe you? Pick all that fit.** (Required)
   - Deaf
   - Hard of hearing
   - Parent or family member of a deaf or hard-of-hearing child
   - Speech therapist (speech-language pathologist) or student
   - Audiologist or student
   - Teacher of deaf and hard-of-hearing students
   - ASL learner or interpreter
   - None of these
3. **If you are deaf or hard of hearing: what do you use? Pick all that fit.**
   - Hearing aids / Cochlear implant / Neither / Prefer not to say
4. **How do you (or your child, or the people you work with) mostly communicate?**
   - Spoken language / Sign language / Both / Other
5. **Have you (or they) ever had speech therapy?**
   - Yes, now / Yes, in the past / No / Prefer not to say
6. **If yes: how was practice between sessions?** (Short answer)
7. **Are there speech sounds that are hard to say because they are hard to hear? Which ones?** (Short answer)
8. **How useful would a tool like this be for you, or for the people you answered for?** (1 = not useful, 5 = very useful)
9. **Would you try it?**
   - Yes / Maybe / No / It is not for me, but I know someone who might
10. **What would make you want to use it? Pick all that fit.**
    - It shows the sound without me needing to hear it
    - The words come from my own hearing test
    - I can practice in private
    - A speech therapist could assign words
    - It is free
    - Nothing
    - Other
11. **What worries you about it? Pick all that fit.**
    - It could be wrong about what I said
    - It could feel like being "fixed"
    - Privacy of my voice or my hearing test
    - Speech practice should be with a professional
    - Hearing people building it without us
    - Nothing
    - Other
12. **Who should pay for a tool like this?**
    - Schools / Speech therapists or clinics / Families / It should be free / Not sure
13. **For ASL learners and signers: would sign shown next to the practice words be welcome, or out of place?** (Welcome / Out of place / Not sure, plus a short answer)
14. **What is missing today in speech practice, or in learning ASL, that you wish existed?** (Long answer)
15. **Anything we should change, or should never say when we present this?** (Long answer)
16. **May we quote your written answers anonymously in our pitch?** (Yes / No)
