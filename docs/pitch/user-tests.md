# User feedback log

Owner: Holly. We ask people for feedback. We do not test people.

## Before each session

Say this, in your own words:

- "We are students building a practice app this weekend. Would you try it for five minutes and tell us what you think? You are helping us. We are not testing you."
- "Your voice stays on the phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded."
- "You can stop at any time."

At the end, ask two things and write down the answers:

1. "May we share what you said in our pitch on Sunday?" (yes, yes without my name, or no)
2. "May we show your before and after numbers?" (yes or no)

If the answer is no, log the session without the quote or the numbers. For anyone under 18, a parent must be there and must answer both questions.

## What to write down

- Who: a first name or a label they choose ("first-year student with hearing aids"). Never a full name.
- Which phone and browser. Quiet room or noisy room.
- What they tried: the hearing check, which sounds, how many words.
- Before and after: the two numbers per sound from the progress screen.
- Where they got stuck, without help from us.
- Any verdict they thought was wrong, and the word. Pass these to Michael.
- Their words about the wording: does anything read as "fixing" them?
- One quote, only with a yes to question 1.
